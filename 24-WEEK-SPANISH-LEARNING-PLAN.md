## CORE PEDAGOGICAL PRINCIPLES

### 1. Krashen's Input Hypothesis: The Foundation

**The Golden Rule:** Language acquisition happens through **comprehensible input** (i + 1), NOT through rules and exercises.

```
i = What you already know
i + 1 = Just slightly above your level (learnable with context clues)
```

**What This Means for You:**
- You'll hear **native-speed Spanish from Day 1** (your brain adapts within weeks)
- **Vocabulary comes before listening** (you see/hear 10 target words first)
- **No grammar rules** (you learn grammar by hearing it repeatedly, not from tables)
- **English translations are scaffolding** (they help you understand, then fade away)

---

### 2. Cognitive Load Theory: Why Simplicity Works

Your working memory can only hold ~7 items at once. Overload it, and learning stops.

**Month 1 Strategy:**
- Audio length: 1-3 minutes per lesson
- New words: 5-10 per lesson (not 30+)
- Grammar focus: Present tense only
- Task difficulty: Binary choices ("Who is speaking?" / "Are they happy?")

**Result:** By end of Week 4, you'll recognize 100+ words that you *hear*.

---

### 3. Spaced Repetition: The Science of Never Forgetting

**The Forgetting Curve:** Without review, you forget 50% of what you learned within 1 day.

**SM-2 Algorithm (Optimal Review Timing):**
- 1st review: 1 day later
- 2nd review: 3 days later
- 3rd review: 7 days later
- 4th+ review: Doubles each time (14 days, 28 days, 56 days, etc.)

**How You Benefit:**
- You'll see vocabulary passively (in lessons/stories) about 20 times before actively reviewing
- Once passive encounters hit 20+, the app auto-adds it to active flashcard review
- No overwhelming deck of 200 cards dumped on you Week 1

---

### 4. Input Before Output: Why Speaking Waits

**The Research:** Your listening comprehension is always ahead of your speaking ability.

**Real Timeline:**
- Month 1: Listening only (your brain adapts to Spanish sounds)
- Month 2: Listening + Reading
- Month 3: Listening + Reading + Shadowing (repeat after native speaker, no pressure)
- Month 3+: Simple AI conversations with scaffolding
- Month 4+: Real conversations with less support

**Why This Works:** By Month 3, you've heard 20+ hours of native Spanish. Speaking is now "retrieving from memory" not "generating from scratch."

## DETAILED WEEK-BY-WEEK BREAKDOWN

### PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (WEEKS 1-4)
**Goal:** Master sound patterns + recognize 100 high-frequency words

#### **WEEK 1: Phonetics Focus**

**What You'll Do:**
1. Learn minimal pairs (Spanish sounds that are easy to confuse)
   - /r/ (rolled) vs /rr/ (trill): "caro" vs "carro"
   - Stressed /ó/ vs unstressed /o/: "pó-lo" vs "po-lo"
   - Spanish /e/ (closed) vs English /ɛ/ (open)

2. Hear native-speed audio repeatedly (your brain will adapt by Day 3)

3. Learn first 20-30 words through **one word at a time**
   - See: Spanish word + picture + "Tap to hear"
   - Do: Tap multiple times until familiar
   - Only show English if you request it

**Words You'll Learn:** Hola, sí, no, yo, tú, él, ella, padre, madre, hermano, hermana, gracias, por favor, de nada

**Effort:** 15-20 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I hear and recognize these 20 words?"

---

#### **WEEK 2: High-Frequency Verbs & Pronouns**

**Lesson Structure (This Pattern Repeats Every Day):**
1. **Vocabulary Preview** (5 min): See 10 target words, hear them, read English definitions
2. **Listening Passage** (3 min): Native-speed audio about daily activities, hear it twice
3. **Comprehension Questions** (3-5 questions): "Who is speaking?" / "What are they doing?"
4. **Story Reading** (10 min): 150-200 word graded story using the same vocabulary
5. **Word Tracking:** Passive encounter counts are logged (silent background process)

**Week 2 Theme: Verbs & Pronouns**
- Vocabulary target: ser (to be), estar (to be/exist), hablar (to speak), yo, tú, él, ella, nosotros
- Lessons: "Who Am I?" dialogue, "Hello, I'm..." practice, "Where Are You?" story
- You'll hear these 10-15 words **again and again** in different contexts

**Effort:** 20-30 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Do I recognize these verbs when I hear them?"  
**Cumulative Words Recognized:** 50+

---

#### **WEEK 3: Common Verbs**

**Week 3 Theme: Daily Activities**
- New vocabulary: hablar (speak), comer (eat), vivir (live), tener (have), ir (go), hacer (do)
- Stories: "Morning Routine" (what people do each day), "Family Activities" (family doing things)
- Week 2 vocabulary (ser, estar, pronouns) repeats naturally in new contexts

**What's Happening Psychologically:**
- Each word you've heard 3-5 times starts to feel familiar (fluency illusion begins)
- Your brain is building recognition patterns without conscious analysis
- By now, native-speed Spanish feels less chaotic; you're tuning into rhythm/patterns

**Effort:** 20-30 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I follow a simple 2-min conversation?"  
**Cumulative Words Recognized:** 75+

---

#### **WEEK 4: Frequent Nouns & Adjectives**

**Week 4 Theme: Descriptions & Objects**
- New vocabulary: casa (house), día (day), agua (water), bueno (good), malo (bad), grande (big)
- Stories combine ALL previous vocabulary: "My House" (objects + descriptions + daily activities)
- Comprehension tasks get slightly harder: "Why did she..." (inference, not just facts)

**Milestone:** End of Week 4, you've consumed ~8-10 hours of native Spanish audio

**What Your Brain Has Done:**
- Adapted to native phonetics (Spanish sounds are now normal)
- Built recognition for 100 high-frequency words
- Developed listening strategies ("guess from context," "focus on familiar words")

**Effort:** 20-30 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I understand the gist of a 3-min conversation about familiar topics?"  
**Cumulative Words Recognized:** 100+

**End of Phase 1 Assessment:**
- ✅ Can recognize 100+ words in listening
- ✅ Can understand basic comprehension questions
- ✅ Comfortable with native-speed audio
- ✅ Ready for Phase 2

### PHASE 2: GROWTH (WEEKS 5-12)
**Goal:** 300-500 recognized words + comfortable with story reading

#### **WEEKS 5-6: Blocked Practice (Same Vocabulary in Similar Contexts)**

**Why Blocked Practice First?**
- Psychology: Blocked practice builds confidence
- Then you switch to interleaved (mixed) practice for deeper learning
- This combination is optimal

---

**WEEK 5: Food & Meals Theme**

**Vocabulary:** comer (eat), beber (drink), pan (bread), agua (water), café (coffee), carne (meat), pollo (chicken), pescado (fish), fruta (fruit), verdura (vegetable)

**3 Lessons:**
1. "Breakfast Dialogue" (2-min conversation about what people eat)
2. "Restaurant Ordering" (how to ask for food)
3. "The Market" (shopping for groceries)

**Story:** "My Favorite Food" (200-300 words about meals, repeats all food vocabulary)

**Daily Pattern:**
- Vocabulary preview: 20 target words (including Week 2-4 words that repeat)
- Listening: 2-3 min passage, hear twice
- Comprehension: 4-5 questions
- Story: 15-20 min reading with click-to-translate
- Passive encounters: Logged (silent)

**What's Changing:** Lessons are slightly longer (2-3 min instead of 1-2 min), vocabulary is larger (20 instead of 10), comprehension harder (inference questions added)

**Effort:** 25-35 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I order food in a restaurant (understood by humans)?"  
**Active Review Deck:** First words hitting 20+ encounters start auto-adding to spaced repetition cards

---

**WEEK 6: Family & Relationships Theme**

**3 Lessons:**
1. "Introducing My Family" (dialogue, 2-3 min)
2. "Family Stories" (short stories about families)
3. "Family in Hispanic Culture" (cultural context, light reading)

**Story:** "My Grandfather" (personal narrative, repeats family vocabulary)

**Interweaving:** Week 5 vocabulary (food) naturally appears in family stories

**Effort:** 25-35 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I tell someone about my family in simple Spanish?"  
**Cumulative Words Recognized:** 200-250

### PHASE 3: CONVERSATION INTRODUCTION (WEEKS 9-12)
**Concurrent with Phase 2** — You keep doing Phase 2 lessons PLUS add speaking activities

**Prerequisite:** 400+ recognized words, 250+ in active review

**Why Now?** Your cognitive load is now manageable. Speaking + listening together doesn't overload your brain anymore.

---

#### **WEEK 9: Shadowing Introduction**

**What is Shadowing?**
- Native speaker says a phrase
- You repeat immediately (listening first, then repeat, no real-time pressure)
- Bridges passive listening and active speaking

**Daily Pattern (In Addition to Phase 2 Lessons):**
1. Listening passage (2-3 min, comprehension as before)
2. Comprehension questions (2-3, binary choice)
3. **NEW: Shadowing Segment**
   - 5-8 phrases from the passage highlighted
   - Click play: Native speaker says phrase slowly first, then natural speed
   - You repeat: Microphone icon records your voice
   - Feedback: "Good pronunciation ✓" or "Let's try again"
4. Self-recording task (optional): Record full sentence, hear playback, compare to native

**Week 9 Lessons:**
- "Greetings & Introductions" (shadowing "Hola, me llamo...")
- "Ordering Food at Restaurant" (shadowing "Quisiera...")
- "Asking for Directions" (shadowing "¿Dónde está...?")

**What You'll Experience:**
```
Native Speaker: "Hola, me llamo Carlos." [Slow]
Native Speaker: "Hola, me llamo Carlos." [Natural Speed]
[Pause for you to shadow]
You (Speaking): "Hola, me llamo Carlos."
App Feedback: "Great stress pattern! ✓"
```

**Effort:** 25-40 min/day × 7 days (includes Phase 2 lesson + shadowing)  
**Progress Check:** "Can I repeat phrases with decent pronunciation?"  
**Cumulative Speaking Practice:** ~30-50 min

---

#### **WEEK 10: AI Conversation with High Scaffolding**

**Scaffolding Level: HIGH**
- AI speaks slowly
- You get 5-10 seconds to think
- Suggestions shown ("Try: 'Me encanta...' or 'No me gusta...'")

**Conversation Scenarios (Tightly Scripted):**
- "Ordering at a Café" (5-7 exchanges, known vocabulary only)
- "Meeting Someone" (5-7 exchanges, introductions & questions)
- "Asking for Directions" (5-7 exchanges, location words)

**Example Conversation:**
```
AI: "¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?"
[You have 10 seconds to answer]
[Suggestions shown: "Me llamo...", "Yo soy..."]
You: "Me llamo Carlos."
AI: "¡Mucho gusto! ¿De dónde eres?"
[You answer]
```

**Daily Pattern:**
- Morning: Phase 2 lesson (25-30 min)
- Evening: One conversation session (10-15 min, 2-3× per week)

**Effort:** 25-45 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I have a 5-exchange conversation without looking at notes?"  
**Cumulative Speaking:** ~1.5-2 hours total

---

#### **WEEK 11: AI Conversation with Medium Scaffolding**

**Scaffolding Level: MEDIUM**
- AI speaks at normal speed
- You get 3-5 seconds to think
- Suggestions still shown, but fewer

**Scenarios Expand Slightly:**
- More realistic conversations (longer exchanges)
- Combining multiple tasks (ordering food + small talk + paying)

**Effort:** 25-45 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I hold a short conversation in real-time?"  
**Cumulative Speaking:** ~3-4 hours total

---

#### **WEEK 12: AI Conversation with Low Scaffolding**

**Scaffolding Level: LOW**
- AI speaks at normal speed
- Real-time responses expected (no think time)
- No suggestions

**Scenarios:**
- More complex exchanges
- Unexpected turns (AI might introduce new related topics)
- More realistic Spanish speakers (different accents/speeds)

**Effort:** 25-45 min/day × 7 days  
**Progress Check:** "Can I understand native-speed Spanish in conversation and respond?"  
**Cumulative Speaking:** ~5-6 hours total

---

**END OF PHASE 3 ASSESSMENT (End Week 12):**

- ✅ 30-40 hours listening

## TIPS FOR MAXIMUM LEARNING

### 1. Consistency > Intensity
- 20 min/day every day beats 2 hours once a week
- Build a habit (same time each day)
- Calendar your learning sessions

### 2. Listen Actively
- Don't just play audio in background
- Pay attention to sounds, patterns, rhythm
- Rewind and listen again if you miss something
- Pause to think about what you heard

### 3. Embrace Incomprehension
- Week 1-2: You'll understand 10-20% (normal!)
- Week 3-4: 20-40% (progress!)
- Week 5-8: 40-70% (exciting!)
- Week 9-12: 70-90% (huge!)
- Remember: You're training your brain, not memorizing

### 4. Story Reading is Gold
- Stories repeat vocabulary in context (powerful for learning)
- Read actively (click to check unfamiliar words)
- Re-read stories you've already read (easier second time, builds confidence)

### 5. Spaced Repetition is Non-Negotiable
- Do your daily flashcard reviews (10-15 min)
- When it's hard, that's when learning happens (don't skip hard cards)
- Trust the algorithm (SM-2 has been researched since 1990)

### 6. Speaking Practice Early
- Shadowing in Week 9 feels awkward (normal!)
- Conversation practice in Week 10 will feel scary (normal!)
- By Week 12, you'll be amazed at what you can say
- Your brain is adapting; give it time

### 7. Embrace Native Speed
- Week 1: Native-speed Spanish sounds fast (very fast)
- Week 2: Still fast, but you catch words
- Week 3: You predict what's coming
- Week 4: Native speed feels normal
- This is why slow audio hurts long-term (don't use it)

### 8. Don't Translate Internally
- Avoid thinking "casa = house" (translation mode)
- Aim for: hear "casa" → see picture → understand "house" (direct meaning)
- This takes time; be patient with yourself

### 9. Celebrate Small Wins
- "I recognized that word!" ✓
- "I understood the first sentence!" ✓
- "I shadowed a phrase perfectly!" ✓
- "I answered a comprehension question right!" ✓
- These are real progress

### 10. Make It Personal
- Choose topics you care about (food you like, places you want to visit)
- By Week 13-24, focus on media that interests you
- Learning is more fun when content matters to you
